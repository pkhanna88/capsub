How confidence is assigned

High
Linguistic cue + acoustic gap both fire near the same timestamp.
Medium

Low
Acoustic gap alone - could be a student moment or a long pause.

A demonstration of the end-to-end CapSub pipeline. Whisper-Small fine-tuned on twenty-four MIT physics lectures. Word-level alignment by WhisperX. Diarization with Pyannote. Custom phonetic post-processor for technical vocabulary. Q&A detection via linguistic patterns, acoustic energy analysis, and speaker labels.
